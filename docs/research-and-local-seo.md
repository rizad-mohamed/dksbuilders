# Focused design, industry and SEO research

Research conducted before implementation, 2 October 2026. This is qualitative search-intent research, not paid keyword-volume research or a promise of rankings.

## Sources and design synthesis

- MAGA: https://maga.lk/ — sector-led capabilities and project evidence support procurement-oriented visitors. Use a clear capability map rather than unsupported superlatives.
- Skanska: https://www.skanska.com/ and https://www.skanska.com/group/en/about-us/how-we-create-value — connect construction expertise, project purpose and reduced customer risk.
- Awwwards Mrozinski Construction: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/mrozinski-construction — disciplined blocks and restrained motion can communicate structural strength.
- Awwwards Blach Construction: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/blach-construction — an editorial corporate treatment can still support clear contractor navigation.
- DKS's four current company pages supplied the factual subject matter. No competitor layouts, photographs, brand marks or code were copied.

Original visual thesis: a strong architectural headline over a labelled concept image; flat charcoal surfaces, DKS blue wayfinding, amber conversion band; numbered capability rows; an archival contact sheet and separate project register; a four-stage line that advances with native scroll. No cursor replacement, scroll interception, invented metrics or animation-gated content.

## Search-intent mapping

| Intent | Home-page coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Construction company / building contractors Sri Lanka | Title, description, company introduction and geographic service area |
| Construction company Elpitiya | Consistent visible address, telephone, email, GeneralContractor structured data |
| Building / house construction | Explicit service rows with readable enquiry links |
| Civil engineering / highway / bridge contractors | Capabilities and multidisciplinary engineering copy |
| Water supply / sewerage / irrigation / drainage contractors | Separate named service rows; no keyword-stuffed repeated paragraphs |
| Contractor portfolio / institutional construction | Verified company project-register excerpts |
| Construction employment | Company careers enquiry; no stale vacancy claims |

Semantic landmarks, a single H1, logical headings, crawlable business information, absolute canonical, Open Graph text metadata, descriptive local asset filenames, alt text, sitemap and robots are implemented. No rating/review schema. Private Sites access limits public indexing until audience/domain decisions are made. Sitemap inclusion does not guarantee indexing.

## Off-page/local SEO plan (separate company work)

1. Verify/claim Google Business Profile with the company owner; confirm official name, address, telephone, hours and service areas. Do not create competing duplicate profiles.
2. Align company website, Google profile and official social accounts to the same NAP. Verify both published inboxes and primary telephone.
3. Confirm current CIDA registration, then link the authoritative record and publish precise approved certificate wording.
4. Add approved project pages with original images, actual scope, dates/status and client permission where needed. Announce project milestones through legitimate company/client channels.
5. Seek relevant industry/member-directory listings and genuine local references. Earn links through documented work and useful engineering content rather than bulk directory submissions.
6. Collect real client reviews without fabricated ratings or incentives tied to sentiment.
7. After public domain launch, verify search-console ownership, submit the final sitemap and monitor queries, indexing, enquiries and field performance.

No profiles, listings, messages or review requests were created or sent in this phase.
